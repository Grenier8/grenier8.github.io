Privacy Policy

Laonion Metrics and Laonion Publisher · Last updated: November 8, 2026

Laonion Metrics and Laonion Publisher are two private tools that the owner of the YouTube channel Cogs & Curves runs on their own computer. Laonion Metrics shows the channel's statistics on a personal dashboard. Laonion Publisher uploads the owner's own videos, after the owner has reviewed and approved them, to the owner's channel. They are not offered to the public: the only user is the channel owner. See the home page for what each tool does.

YouTube API Services

Both tools use YouTube API Services. By connecting them, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data they access

Only after the owner signs in with Google and grants access, and only for the owner's own channel (each tool rejects access to any other channel). Each tool has its own authorization.

Laonion Metrics (read-only)

It requests two read-only scopes: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly.

  • YouTube Data API v3: channel ID and name, subscriber count, total views, and the channel's uploaded videos (ID, title, publish date, duration and view count).
  • YouTube Analytics API: aggregate statistics for the channel and its videos, by day and by format (long videos or Shorts): views, watch time, average percentage viewed, and subscribers gained and lost.
  • YouTube Reporting API: the channel's daily reach report (thumbnail impressions and click-through rate). To receive it, the tool creates one reporting job for the channel; this does not change the channel or its videos.
  • An OAuth access token, so it can keep reading these statistics without asking the owner to sign in every time.

It does not access personal information about viewers: YouTube Analytics data is aggregated. Laonion Metrics cannot upload, edit, delete or publish anything.

Laonion Publisher (uploads)

It requests one scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl, which YouTube requires to upload caption files and add videos to playlists. It uses it only for the actions listed here.

  • What it sends to YouTube: the owner's own approved video files and their title, description, tags, category, language, privacy status or scheduled publish time, made-for-kids setting and AI-use disclosure; the video's custom thumbnail; its English caption file; and the playlist it belongs to.
  • What it reads from YouTube: the channel's ID (to confirm it is the owner's channel), and, for the videos it uploaded, their ID and their upload, processing and privacy status (to confirm that each upload worked and that scheduled videos were published). It also lists the channel's recent uploads to avoid uploading the same video twice.
  • An OAuth access token, separate from Laonion Metrics' token, so it can upload without asking the owner to sign in every time.

It never deletes videos, never changes a video's privacy status without the owner's instruction and never uploads anything the owner has not approved. It does not read or write comments, ratings, subscriptions or other channels' data, and it does not access information about viewers.

How the data is used

Only to show the owner's channel statistics on the owner's personal dashboard, to track the channel's progress over time, and to upload and schedule the owner's own approved videos and confirm that they were published. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

Storage and sharing

  • The data and the tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in local files. They are not uploaded to any server or cloud service other than YouTube itself (for the owner's own videos).
  • Laonion Publisher keeps, in the owner's local production records, the ID, link, scheduled time and upload status of each video it uploaded.
  • The data is not shared, sold or transferred to anyone. There are no third parties: no third-party analytics, advertising or content.
  • The tokens are never displayed or written to logs. The tools talk to Google only over HTTPS and the dashboard is reachable only from the owner's own computer.
  • Neither the tools nor these pages use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

How long the data is kept

The statistics are kept while the owner uses Laonion Metrics, because the dashboard shows how the channel changes over time. The tool refreshes them several times a day with the owner's active authorization. Laonion Publisher's upload records are kept while the owner uses it, and the status of each uploaded video is checked again at least every 30 days while it is kept. If access to a tool is revoked or the tool stops being used, its token and all YouTube data it stored are deleted from the computer within 30 days. The owner can delete them at any time.

How to revoke access

Open your Google Account's security settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (also reachable at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions), select Laonion Metrics or Laonion Publisher and remove its access. From then on that tool cannot read or upload anything. Each tool is revoked separately.
