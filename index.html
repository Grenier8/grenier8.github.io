Laonion tools

Two private tools for one YouTube channel: Laonion Metrics and Laonion Publisher.

The owner of the YouTube channel Cogs & Curves runs these tools on their own computer. They are not offered to the public: the only user is the channel owner, and they work only with the owner's own channel, after the owner signs in with Google and grants access.

Laonion Metrics

A read-only statistics tool.

  • Shows subscribers, views, watch time, average percentage viewed, and thumbnail impressions and click-through rate for the owner's channel and its videos.
  • Uses the YouTube Data API v3, the YouTube Analytics API and the YouTube Reporting API with read-only access (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly).
  • It cannot upload, edit, delete, comment on or publish anything on YouTube.

Laonion Publisher

An upload tool for the owner's own, already approved videos.

  • Uploads a video that the owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's channel, with its title, description, tags, category, language, made-for-kids setting and AI-use disclosure.
  • Lets the owner choose the privacy status (public, private or unlisted) or a scheduled publish time.
  • Sets the video's custom thumbnail, uploads its English caption file and adds it to one of the channel's playlists.
  • Checks the upload's processing and publishing status for that video.
  • Uses the YouTube Data API v3 with the youtube.force-ssl scope.
  • It never deletes videos, never changes a video's privacy status without the owner's instruction, and never uploads anything the owner has not approved. It does not read or write comments, ratings or subscriptions.

What neither tool does

  • They do not access other people's channels or accounts.
  • They do not share, sell or send data to third parties: everything stays on the owner's computer.
  • They do not use cookies, ads or trackers.
