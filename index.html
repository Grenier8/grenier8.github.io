Laonion Metrics

A private, read-only statistics tool for one YouTube channel.

Laonion Metrics reads the YouTube statistics of its owner's own channel, Cogs & Curves, and shows them on a personal dashboard. It runs on the owner's computer and is not offered to the public: the only user is the channel owner.

What it does

  • Shows subscribers, views, watch time, average percentage viewed, and thumbnail impressions and click-through rate for the owner's channel and its videos.
  • Uses the YouTube Data API v3, the YouTube Analytics API and the YouTube Reporting API with read-only access (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Reads data only from the owner's channel, and only after the owner signs in with Google and grants access.
  • Keeps the data on the owner's computer. Nothing is shared, sold or sent to third parties.

What it does not do

  • It cannot upload, edit, delete, comment on or publish anything on YouTube.
  • It does not access other people's channels or accounts.
  • It does not use cookies, ads or trackers.
